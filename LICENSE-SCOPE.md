## Future reference implementation

The approved plan is Apache License 2.0 for future reference-implementation source code. No Safehouse reference implementation exists in this package. This plan does not convert documents to Apache-2.0, license unrelated existing code, or automatically relicense future third-party dependencies. When code is introduced, its paths and license notices must clearly identify its Apache-2.0 scope.

## Exclusions

YueYun branding, logos and White Paper 3.0 are not automatically included in these grants. A textual project-name attribution does not grant trademark rights or official endorsement. Their permissions require separate decisions. CC BY 4.0 itself does not license patent or trademark rights.

Private runtime data, personal memories, credentials, internal audit archives and unrelated YueYun engineering projects are outside this package and are not offered through this document.
