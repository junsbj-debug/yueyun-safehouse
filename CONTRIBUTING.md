# Contributing

This initiative is maintained by one initiator. There is no elected council, foundation, voting rule, guaranteed response time or established escalation body. Governance and use of the YueYun/Safehouse names remain undecided. A contribution does not establish endorsement or authority to claim compatibility.

Please use one of the issue templates for a research question, threat-model proposal or fictional test case. Separate current behavior, proposed requirements and evidence. State the version, assumptions, expected failure behavior and whether a test was actually run. Do not mark a proposed or unexecuted test PASS.

Keep changes small and attributable. Protocol, trust-boundary, licensing and governance decisions need explicit maintainer approval. Do not modify historical evidence to improve results. Independent replication is welcome but has not yet been demonstrated for Safehouse.

Use invented data. Do not post personal memories, identifiable conversations, credentials, internal tickets, raw Context or runtime snapshots. Security concerns follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issue reproduction.

The public documents and specification explicitly included in [LICENSE-SCOPE.md](LICENSE-SCOPE.md) are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE). Future reference-implementation code is planned for Apache License 2.0; its scope and notices will be established at code release. YueYun branding, logos and White Paper 3.0 are not automatically included, and this decision does not license all future materials. The source draft's Apache-2.0 suggestion remains historical context, not the document license. No CLA or DCO has been adopted. Authors should identify third-party material and its permissions; do not assume a license across the whole YueYun system.
